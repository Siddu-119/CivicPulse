# CivicPulse V2 — Official Hackathon Demonstration Narration Script
Project: CivicPulse V2
Target Duration: 3 minutes 35 seconds (215 seconds)
Live Frontend: https://civicpulse-governance.vercel.app/
Live Backend: https://civicpulse-backend-pill.onrender.com
GitHub: https://github.com/Siddu-119/CivicPulse

================================================================================
[Opening — 0:00 - 0:15]
"Cities face everyday challenges: damaged roads, overflowing waste, waterlogging, and public infrastructure problems. But reporting these issues and ensuring they reach the right authorities can be difficult.

Introducing CivicPulse V2 — a smart civic complaint and resolution platform designed to connect citizens with municipal authorities."

================================================================================
[Platform overview — 0:15 - 0:40]
"CivicPulse provides a centralized digital platform where citizens can report civic problems, follow complaint progress, and view reported issues through an interactive map.

Its goal is simple: make civic issue reporting more accessible, transparent, and organized."

================================================================================
[Citizen experience — 0:40 - 1:10]
"Citizens can access the platform through its authentication system and their personal dashboard.

The dashboard provides an overview of reported complaints and their statuses, helping citizens keep track of the issues they have raised."

================================================================================
[Smart classification — 1:10 - 1:50]
"One of CivicPulse's key features is its rule-based smart classification system.

When a civic issue is described, the system analyzes relevant keywords and patterns to identify its category, estimate priority, and recommend the appropriate department.

For example, a report describing a hazardous pothole can be classified under roads and potholes, with high priority and a suggested department.

This helps organize incoming complaints and supports more consistent routing."

================================================================================
[Interactive map — 1:50 - 2:20]
"CivicPulse also includes an interactive map powered by Leaflet and OpenStreetMap.

Citizens and administrators can explore the geographic distribution of civic issues and view complaint markers and categories.

This location-based view helps make reported problems easier to understand."

================================================================================
[Complaint tracking — 2:20 - 2:50]
"Transparency is another important part of the platform.

Through the public tracking portal, users can check complaint progress using a tracking code without needing to navigate the entire dashboard.

The platform also supports complaint updates, notifications, and citizen feedback."

================================================================================
[Administration — 2:50 - 3:15]
"On the administrative side, CivicPulse provides tools for reviewing complaints, assigning them to the appropriate workflow, monitoring resolution status, and viewing analytics.

These capabilities are part of the implemented application. This demonstration presents the administrative workflow using verified project evidence."

================================================================================
[Technology and closing — 3:15 - 3:35]
"CivicPulse V2 is built using React, TypeScript, Java, Spring Boot, Spring Security, JWT authentication, and TiDB Cloud Serverless.

The frontend is deployed on Vercel, while the backend runs on Render.

CivicPulse brings together technology, transparency, and civic participation to support smarter urban governance.

CivicPulse V2 — making every civic issue count.

Thank you."
